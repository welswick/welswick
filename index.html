About
Wyatt

Wyatt Elswick (B. 2004) is a painter working in San Francisco and Los Angeles. His paintings occupy a space between observation and invention; where figures, narratives, and landscapes emerge from and recede into heavily worked painted surfaces.

His work has recently been featured in Heavy Batter: A Show on Absurdity at Cushion Works gallery.
